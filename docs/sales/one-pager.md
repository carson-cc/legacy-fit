# Veltro — One-Pager

## What it is

Veltro is a behavioral assessment platform built for executive search firms. It gives recruiters a
structured, data-driven view of how a candidate naturally operates — their dominant behavioral
style, their fit against a target profile, and how they're likely to interact with the hiring
team — in about 10 minutes of candidate time and zero recruiter calibration.

The output is a scored report: a behavioral archetype, four dimension percentiles, a fit percentage
against the search target, and a team-dynamics note. Recruiters use it to move from gut-feel
shortlists to defensible, documented recommendations.

## Who it's for

Veltro is purpose-built for **retained executive search firms** that:

- Run searches at the VP, SVP, C-suite, and board level
- Deliver a formal shortlist and written recommendation to their client
- Need to differentiate their process from contingency competitors
- Want a behavioral data point they can cite in the shortlist debrief — without building or
  licensing a full assessment suite themselves

It is **not** for high-volume hourly hiring, ATS replacement, or HR teams running their own hiring.

## How it works

1. **Target setup** — the recruiter completes a 5-minute behavioral profile for the ideal
   candidate, using the same adjective inventory. This sets the target against which candidates
   are scored.

2. **Candidate invitation** — the recruiter sends an invitation link (one click from the dashboard).
   The candidate receives a branded email and completes the assessment in 8–12 minutes.

3. **Scored report** — Veltro scores the candidate instantly. The recruiter sees a behavioral
   profile, fit percentage, dimension breakdown, and team-dynamics note. The hiring manager can
   be shared a white-labeled portal with the shortlist.

4. **Client portal** — the recruiting firm can share a curated shortlist with the hiring
   organization via a magic-link portal. Candidates are shown as approved or flagged; the client
   can advance or pass candidates and leave feedback.

## Three proof points

**1. 2.245 million norm sample.** Candidate scores are percentile-ranked against a normative
database of 2.245 million profiles collected across industries and geographies over multiple
decades. No firm-specific calibration required.

**2. DISC-grounded, built for speed.** The forced-choice adjective inventory reduces social
desirability bias and takes 8–12 minutes — fast enough that candidates don't drop off. The scoring
model is rooted in the DISC framework, backed by 60+ years of applied use and meta-analytic support
(Barrick & Mount, 1991; Schmidt & Hunter, 1998).

**3. Fully white-labeled client portal.** Hiring organizations see the recruiting firm's brand, not
Veltro's. The portal shows only approved candidates (stripped of recruiter notes) with Advance /
Pass controls and report access. It replaces the PDF-email shortlist with a living, interactive
interface.
