## Slide 2 — The Problem

**Executive search runs on gut feel — and firms can't prove it.**

- A typical retained search produces a shortlist of 3–5 candidates. The rationale is a written bio
  and a recruiter's read.
- Hiring organizations increasingly ask "how do you know?" Firms that can't answer with data lose
  mandates to those that can.
- Existing psychometric tools (Hogan, SHL, Korn Ferry Architect) cost $300–$800 per assessment,
  require certification, and produce 40-page reports no one reads.

*The market wants structured behavioral data. It doesn't want a psychology test.*

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

**Veltro: 10 minutes of candidate time → a defensible hiring recommendation.**

- Candidate completes an 8–12 minute forced-choice adjective inventory
- Veltro scores against 2.245M norm sample → behavioral profile + fit % vs. role target
- Recruiter gets a one-page report they can present confidently
- Hiring org gets a white-labeled portal to review, advance, or pass candidates

*It replaces the gut-feel shortlist, not the recruiter.*

## Slide 7 — Methodology

**2.245M norm sample. DISC-grounded. Honest about what we don't claim.**

| What we say | What we don't say |
|---|---|
| Scored against 2.245M profiles | Independently validated for your specific role |
| Rooted in DISC, backed by meta-analysis | A standalone hiring decision |
| Reduces social desirability bias vs. Likert | Zero adverse-impact risk |
| Consistent across industries without recalibration | A replacement for structured interviews |

*Barrick & Mount (1991); Schmidt & Hunter (1998) establish the class of tools. We're transparent that Veltro itself hasn't been independently criterion-validated yet.*

## Slide 8 — Market

**Retained executive search is a $15B+ global market. We start with the mid-market.**

- ~3,000 independent retained search firms in the US with 5–50 recruiters
- Target: boutique and regional firms that run 20–100 searches/year
- They cannot afford Korn Ferry Architect or Hogan. They can't build their own assessment.
- They are already paying for generic DISC tools (Wiley, Extended DISC) — we replace those with
  a search-workflow-native product

## Slide 9 — Business Model

**SaaS platform fee + per-assessment fee. No certification. No implementation fee.**

| Tier | Platform fee | Per-assessment | Target customer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design Partner | $500/mo | $200 | 5 firms, 12-month, structured feedback |
| Boutique | $1,500/mo | $150 | 5–20 recruiter firm |
| Firm | $4,500/mo | $100 | 20+ recruiter firm, white-label portal |

**Unit economics (Boutique, 30 assessments/mo):**
- Platform: $1,500
- Assessments: 30 × $150 = $4,500
- Monthly revenue per customer: **$6,000**
- COGS (hosting + AI + email): ~$80/mo
- Gross margin: >98%
